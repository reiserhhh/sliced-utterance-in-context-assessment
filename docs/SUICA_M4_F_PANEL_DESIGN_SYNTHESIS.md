## 1. The question the line inherited

M4-E1 found the **saturated yardstick**: on real text, the deployed relation
field's internal split-half agreement is nil at every budget under every
estimator convention, and the two conventions are near-projectively
equivalent, so the binding constraint is not the estimator but the panel. V8's
route index already required a fresh D3 panel with its event count and
composition registered before opening. The line's question was therefore
concrete: **what panel would certify a relation field, and can one be built?**

## 3. The result

**A finite panel can be made self-consistent, and cannot be made
trait-certifying.**

- Scale alone does nothing affordable (F1).
- Composition — observing authors on shared occasions — works, and works at
  fixed budget (F2).
- More text per author is useless and eventually harmful; more authors is the
  live axis, with a clean, out-of-sample-validated power law (F3, F4).
- But what that axis certifies is an **occasion-bound, state-inclusive**
  configuration. The trait-like object it looks like it is approaching, it is
  not approaching: long-window recovery plateaus near .15 while the gauge runs
  to .39 (F5).
- And the classical remedy — spreading observation across decorrelated
  occasions — does not close the gap either, on a test that is both causally
  live and adequately powered (F9), with the gauge provably blind to that axis
  (F6, replicated F7/F9).

This is the foundation gap ledger's **F10 (state/trait depends on the occasion
universe and horizon)** appearing as a measurement rather than a caveat, and
it hardens V8's typing discipline: the relation field a panel certifies is a
**P-type object indexed by its occasion universe**, and no amount of panel
engineering promotes it to a trait-level claim.

**Standing consequence.** D3 is permanently restricted to occasion-bound
objects. The trait-level route, if one exists, is not on the panel side; the
M4-E2 objective-redesign line is the only remaining direction.

**Scope.** Everything above is measured in synthetic worlds calibrated to the
real-text regime, through the deployed frozen map. It licenses design priors
and refusals, not claims about any corpus, construct, person, or diagnosis.

## 4. What the line cost in method, and what it bought

Four planner registration defects of one family occurred, were caught, and are
recorded in the plan document rather than repaired away:

1. **F4 / G0** — a designed-null gate whose text mixed a per-cell rule with a
   trend rule. → Rule: designed-null gates state their aggregation rule and
   multiplicity treatment before the run.
2. **F6** — the base scale was left free and no power statement was required,
   so a null was measured at the target's noise floor. → Rule: every leg
   pre-states the scale at which its target is measurably non-zero and its
   minimum detectable effect; a noise-floor null is UNDERPOWERED, not a null.
3. **F7** — a design dimension was economized away without checking which of
   its values carried the causal channel, justified by citing the arm that was
   in fact the underpowered one. → Rule: verify a non-zero causal channel at
   every tested parameter value from the generator; a null on an inert knob is
   VACUOUS.
4. **F8** — a gate written as a nil-significance test on a residual that is
   nonzero by construction (phi^41 = 1.06e-4), so its failure probability grew
   with sample size; at 16x the authors it voided a leg on correlations of a
   few e-4 against effects of .03–.08. → Rule, subsuming the others: **every
   gate bounds MATERIALITY via an equivalence form — the confidence interval
   must lie inside a margin justified from the generator or the effect size —
   never nil significance on a known-nonzero quantity.**

Two behaviours by executing agents set the standard the line now holds itself
to, and both are on the record: M4-F7's agent disclosed that its own
adequately-powered null might be structurally inert **before** the planner
found it in the generator; M4-F8's agent computed the reading that would have
delivered a clean closure, declined it because the registered rule said VOID,
and published the declined numbers so the refusal could be audited.

The honesty record for the arc: registered pivots fired as pre-committed in
F3, F5, F6, F7 and F9; F4's saturation pivot correctly did not fire; F2's
pivot did not fire while its measured law still decided the question; F8
voided; two planner generalizations (F3's and F6's pivot consequences) were
suspended by later evidence and said so in writing; one recommendation
(26,052 authors) was withdrawn by the audit that was registered to kill it.
