# SUICA M3 Two-Phase Microkernel Independent Audit

Decision: `PARTIAL`

## Ruling

The V2 discovery is a meaningful correction over the V1 matched-family
baseline, but it is not a general micro-to-meso closure and must not be called
an out-of-family event-kernel confirmation.

## What survives audit

1. The \(L^2(Q_0)\) definitions of \(G_u\), \(a_u\),
   \(B_u^{\mathrm{proj}}\), and \(h_u\) are coherent. They are explicitly
   public-coordinate projections, not planted generator coefficients.
2. The free phase now contains a genuine event-level joint \((S_t,C_t)\)
   chain with exact joint-stationary oracle targets.
3. Training-occasion-selected hierarchical shrinkage separates shared
   sequence information from author-specific transition information.
   Across positive worlds, minimum mean held-out personal transition skill is
   `0.0405`; the null-author world is `-0.0000019`.
4. Public response projection, relative occasion state, and independent-view
   author response geometry are recoverable in the registered smooth
   synthetic worlds.
5. The stable-position/state alias now passes through the estimator:
   maximum observable error is `3.33e-16`, minimum alternative-truth
   difference is `0.989`, and both decompositions trigger the single-occasion
   state refusal.

## Why the conjunction remains partial

1. Generator and estimator are basis-separated but near-family. Gaussian
   random-Fourier response functions are a finite approximation to the
   Gaussian RBF family used by the estimator.
2. Nonlinear remainder correlation does not imply nonlinear predictive gain.
   Held-out incremental \(R^2\) is:
   - P2: `-0.0097`, 95% CI `[-0.0333, 0.0069]`, 6/12 positive seeds;
   - P3: `+0.0348`, 95% CI `[0.0148, 0.0574]`, 11/12 positive seeds;
   - P4: `-0.0032`, 95% CI `[-0.0140, 0.0084]`, 5/12 positive seeds.
   General nonlinear predictive improvement is therefore not established.
3. Several refusals are protocol-interface checks. They prove that declared
   version, support, randomization, reliability, and coarsening metadata
   constrain the license. They do not prove that the estimator can infer
   unlogged MNAR, dependence, or population drift from values alone.
4. This is a 12-seed discovery tuned after exploratory failures. It is not a
   sealed fresh-seed confirmation.
5. Choice events and fixed vector responses are observed in separate phases.
   The correct name is a two-phase microkernel, not a single fully observed
   \(K^S K^C K^X\) event stream.

## Licensed statement

Under registered shared-support, fixed-reference, repeated-occasion smooth
synthetic worlds, a two-phase design can recover author choice occupancy,
shared and author-specific transition information, public-coordinate response
projection, within-author relative occasion state, and independent-occasion
author response geometry. This licenses neither general nonlinear recovery,
human-text existence, personality meaning, construct validity, nor clinical
use.

## Minimum confirmation redesign

1. Freeze code, thresholds, and a new seed schedule before truth is generated;
   hash predictions before opening oracle targets.
2. Add generators outside the Gaussian kernel family: spline/piecewise,
   Voronoi/discontinuous, and random non-kernel MLP response fields.
3. Require held-out personal transition skill versus pooled transition to
   have a 95% CI lower bound above zero; separately retain shared-order and
   null-personal gates.
4. Require held-out nonlinear incremental \(R^2\) to have a positive 95% CI
   lower bound and a predeclared positive-seed fraction.
5. Inject actual MNAR, technical dependence, mixed-condition coarsening, and
   reference-population shift worlds. Keep protocol refusal and statistical
   detection claims separate.
6. Keep the macro interpretation map \(\Psi\) closed until real-text
   persistence and external psychological or behavioral anchoring are tested.
