# V8.3.7F Independent Methodology Audit

Ruling: `PASS_WITH_MAJOR_CAVEATS`

This audit accepts the registered synthetic decision. It does not promote the
method to a human-text score or personality interpretation.

## Accepted

- The V3.7E parent seal and six-file V3.7F prospective content seal verified.
- Router-reference, zero-reference, calibration, and evaluation authors were
  disjoint in every canonical world.
- Evaluation authors did not define the router, origin, rank, or basis.
- All 11,790 recorded RNG streams were unique.
- The canonical output had the registered 600 main and 120 uncertainty rows.
- The author-permutation control selected rank 0 and returned residual AUC
  0.499.
- The exact-rank scorer-only residual returned chance AUC, while the
  dense-tail residual was strongly detectable.
- Event and full-pipeline Monte Carlo regions met the frozen coverage gates.
- A same-seed full rerun was byte-identical for all nine core outputs.

## Major caveats

### Coverage is an oracle synthetic calibration

Fresh event panels are drawn from the scorer-known synthetic probability
process. Bias, covariance, and radius are calibrated and tested on disjoint
Monte Carlo draws. This validates the region machinery under the planted
DGP; it is not yet an observable-only bootstrap or deployable confidence
interval.

### The exact ANOVA reconstruction gate is algebraic

Balanced functional ANOVA reconstructs the resampling tensor by construction.
The near-zero error verifies code consistency, not that the labels
`reference`, `selection`, and `event` exhaust uncertainty in human text.

### The information-conserving rule is not universally superior

In dense-tail worlds the registered full-space-or-soft rule reduced capacity
floor by 35%. In the exact rank-12 world, however, soft weighting increased
finite-sample total NRMSE relative to the selected hard score. The rule is a
dense-spectrum mechanism result, not a universal production estimator.

### Adaptive residual and scorer-only residual differ

The scorer-known exact basis produced residual AUC 0.498, but the estimated
rank-12 residual was 0.551. Finite calibration leaves stable information
outside the estimated basis even when the DGP is exactly low rank. This
supports retaining the unresolved channel, but it also means a deployed
estimator cannot certify its own residual as noise.

### Zero is external, not error-free

The origin is independent of calibration and evaluation authors but is still
estimated from 256 finite norm authors. The reported 74.6% scaling result
concerns external-zero variance when panel size increases from 64 to 256; it
is not a complete reference-population transport theorem.

### The asymptotic statements are practical, not mathematical identities

The fitted event floor uses five budgets and a three-parameter curve. Its
upper bound below .02 supports practical equivalence to zero in this DGP; it
does not prove an exact zero limit. The positive state floor is deliberately
planted and proves possibility, not a universal human lower bound.

### Favorable synthetic family

The exact-rank control removes context and author-by-context terms to preserve
an exact 12-dimensional ILR span. Dense worlds use one power-law spectrum,
one state-correlation mechanism, balanced opportunity, and common reference
support. Nonlinear operators, missing support, MNAR opportunity, reference
mixture drift, and real text remain open.

### Author information is not personality

Residual AUC measures same-author structure against hard negatives. It may
represent stable choice, style, topic policy, context response, or other
author-linked behavior. No psychological construct or clinical validity is
established.

## Final ruling

V3.7F closes the registered synthetic question: event noise can become
practically negligible while total uncertainty remains positive, and hard
projection can omit stable distributed author information. It justifies
replacing deletion-oriented denoising with reliability-weighted,
information-preserving decomposition.

The next mathematical task should be an observable-only error protocol and a
risk-selected conserving estimator that is noninferior in both exact-low-rank
and dense-spectrum worlds. Human-text existence and psychological validity
remain separate later gates.
