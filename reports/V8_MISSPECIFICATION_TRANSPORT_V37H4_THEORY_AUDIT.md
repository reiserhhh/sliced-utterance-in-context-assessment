## Why the frozen STOP is binding

The registered W0 gate required the one-sided 95% upper bound on the
familywise false-refusal probability to be strictly below .05. Both noise
modes produced 2/100 refusals, with upper bound .06162. The gate failed and
cannot be changed after observing the result.

The failure is one of certification, not evidence of inflation. A 2% point
estimate is compatible with a true rate of 5%, and the 100-repetition design
had low probability of passing the strict bound even if the true rate were
2%. The correct statement is "calibration below 5% remains unresolved."

## Prospective repair

The next study must be a new W0-only calibration rather than a reinterpretation
of H.4.

- Use fresh seeds and 1,000 repetitions per noise mode.
- Keep the detector unchanged.
- Control the two-noise family with simultaneous one-sided 97.5% binomial
  intervals.
- Return `CALIBRATED` only when both upper bounds are below .05.
- Return `MISCALIBRATED` only when a lower bound is at least .05.
- Return `INCONCLUSIVE` otherwise.
- Freeze the exact-binomial power table before execution.

If the number of permutations is increased from 99 to 999, that is a new
detector and must be calibrated as a separate method.
