1. detector sensitivity;
2. the number and energy of active authors that happen to enter the test

- Gaussian missed cells: mean 3.86;
- \(t_5\) detected cells: mean 6.79;
- \(t_5\) missed cells: mean 3.75.

expectation, versus approximately 1.06 in detected cells.

change.
