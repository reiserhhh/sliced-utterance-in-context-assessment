# V8 R2G.1 Independent Mathematical Audit

Date: 2026-07-27

Ruling:
`PARTIAL_CONSTRUCTIVE_EXISTENCE`

The numerical run is valid. The machine gate passed every registered
endpoint, but the original `FACTOR_COMPLETENESS_LIMIT` wording exceeded what
the design identifies.

## Accepted

1. In the registered score-sufficient linear and quadratic worlds, finite
   score measurement error creates a non-zero within-group residual floor.
2. Increasing independent score opportunities reduces that floor on fresh
   groups.
3. At \(K=64\) and the noiseless-score endpoint, both bounded model families
   are inside the registered practical-zero region.
4. The pure-iid control remains null.
5. The score-unavailable common shock remains near `.10` at every \(K\).
6. The overfit control from R2G reaches training \(R^2=1\) but approximately
   \(-1.02\) on confirmation.

For all four `completable world x noise` cells, every one of the 30 paired
roots had a lower floor at the noiseless-score endpoint than at \(K=4\).
The exact one-sided sign-test value is \(2^{-30}=9.31\times10^{-10}\) per
cell (`3.73e-9` after a four-cell Bonferroni multiplication). This is a
post-run robustness calculation, not a substitute for the omitted
prospective simultaneous inference procedure.

## Required corrections

### Noiseless score is not exact zero

Machine value `score_opportunities=0` means:

\[
K_s=\infty,\qquad
\text{score measurement noise}=0.
\]

Target resolution remains \(K_t=4\). Independent target noise and individual
residual energy remain. The result concerns the common aggregation floor,
not total residual energy.

### Group floor is not an invariant society floor

The experiment increases authors within a planted group. Its fixed component
is \(b_{\mathrm{group}}\). If \(G\) independent groups are also averaged:

\[
M(G,n)
=
b_{\mathrm{soc}}
+\frac{b_{\mathrm{group}}}{G}
+\frac{a_{\mathrm{author}}}{Gn}.
\]

R2G.1 does not identify \(b_{\mathrm{soc}}\).

### Completion and unavailable structure may interact

The correct common residual is:

\[
b_{\mathrm{joint}}(K,m)
=
E\|U+\Delta^c_{K,m}\|^2
=
b_U+b_\Delta(K,m)
+2E\langle U,\Delta^c_{K,m}\rangle.
\]

The cross term cannot be omitted without an orthogonality assumption.

### Training-support infinity was not tested

The registered supports were 32 and 128 training groups. The direct result is:

\[
b_\Delta(K_s=\infty,m=128)
<\delta_{\mathrm{practical}},
\]

not an empirical demonstration of \(m\to\infty\).

## Statistical cautions

- Root quantiles are not confidence intervals for the mean or registered
  tolerance bounds.
- The machine gate is endpointwise and lacks simultaneous multiplicity
  correction.
- The paired \(K=4\) versus \(K=\infty\) contrast should be the primary
  resolution statistic in any paper-grade rerun.
- The fitted finite-range exponents have no formal interval.
- Nested group-size points do not satisfy the independent-observation
  interpretation of ordinary AICc; the fixed \(b+a/n\) estimand, rather than
  model-selection AICc, should remain primary.

## Final claim

> R2G.1 provides a constructive synthetic existence result: when a world is
> generated by a shared factor that is contained in the registered bounded
> score-to-target function class, finite score resolution can create a
> non-zero within-group residual floor. Increasing independent score
> opportunities removes that floor on fresh groups, while a common component
> absent from the score information set does not improve. This does not
> establish exact zero, unknown-factor discovery, real-text completeness,
> social-scale zero, or psychological meaning.
