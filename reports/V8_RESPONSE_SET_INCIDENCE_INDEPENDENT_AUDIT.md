# V8 Response-Set Incidence Independent Audit

Decision: `PARTIAL`.

The executable registered gate passed. The scientific audit does not promote
that result beyond low-noise affine planted templates.

## Passed

- \(S_u\) and \(\Gamma_u\) are kept distinct.
- Same-condition and free-image optimization use the intended shared versus
  independent condition coordinates.
- Calibration, confirmation, sensitivity, dimension, and graph seed blocks
  are disjoint.
- Confirmation does not select thresholds.
- Private condition identity fails closed.
- Manifest and artifact inventory verify against the audited output package.

## Findings that limit the claim

1. AUC 1.0 is an in-template Monte Carlo result with a large positive/negative
   gap, not fresh-template generalization.
2. The current `excess` graph is a near-equality graph over affine maps,
   not yet population excess incidence relative to a condition-matched null.
3. Its raw Frobenius operator distance is not invariant to condition scaling
   or a change of condition basis.
4. Persistence over tube radii, transversality rank, intersection dimension,
   and tangency order are not implemented.
5. Dimension recovery refits the correctly specified affine family and then
   samples that fitted map. It is a finite-scale estimator check.
6. Common rigid transforms test an algebraic identity, not empirical
   robustness after refitting transformed observations.
7. Noise at 0.06 and above triggers universal refusal for ordinary worlds.
8. Current attacks do not cover partial overlap, correlated noise, missing
   conditions, nonlinear misspecification, or consistent condition
   misregistration.
9. The repository was dirty and the new files were untracked when run. The
   hashes prove artifact-to-code binding but not an externally sealed
   preregistration.
10. The finite direction families are not Kakeya sets. Their finite-scale
    union slopes do not establish full dimension or zero measure.

## Accepted wording

> In fixed low-noise, densely shared, correctly specified affine planted
> templates, calibration-only rules on independent noise seeds distinguished
> same-condition from free-image incidence and refused specified unstable
> conditions. This validates a technical response-set incidence kernel, not
> cross-template generalization. The current graph result measures
> near-equivalence of whole affine response maps. Nonlinear response sets,
> persistent incidence, condition-matched population excess, real-text
> structure, and psychological interpretation remain unvalidated.
