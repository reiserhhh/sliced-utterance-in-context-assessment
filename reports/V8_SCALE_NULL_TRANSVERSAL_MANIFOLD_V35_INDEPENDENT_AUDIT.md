## Not accepted as general claims

1. `nonlinear_auc=1.0` is not general nonlinear-manifold recovery. The
   positive class contains nonlinear transverse sheets, the negative class
   contains tangent/coincident sheets, and the score is the same first-order
   rank diagnostic used to define transversality.
2. `intersection_dimension_mae=0.0` is not an independent unknown-dimension
   estimator. The classifier assigns dimension 1 to the transverse surface
   template and 2 to the coincident template after applying the same local
   rank rule.
3. The local coordinate origin and low-noise polynomial neighborhood are
   supplied by the simulator. Unknown contact localization, rough embedding
   paths, sparse sampling, and arbitrary manifold geometry remain untested.
4. The internal content-hash seal was made on a dirty worktree. It provides
   useful provenance but is not an externally immutable preregistration.
