- the three worlds share labels, cues, direction libraries, node geometry,

The static-marginal accuracy was 0.316. The reproducible V3.6.1 audit gave a
repetition-cluster bootstrap 95% CI [0.293, 0.337], permutation null mean
0.334, and `p=0.903`. This supports marginal matching for the 15 registered
summaries, not for every possible static statistic.

## Scope corrections

1. The per-step Blahut-Arimoto calculation pools stage, incoming branch, and
   group. It is then multiplied across stages under an independent-channel
   assumption. `27/27 addressable leaves` is largely entailed by the
   deterministic \(B^+=C\) generator and enumeration of every cue path. It is
   not evidence of planning, generalization, or general tree-search capacity.
2. The frozen plan requested cue-conditioned whole-path entropy, but the
   runner did not emit it. The post-seal V3.6.1 scope audit now reports it,
   including permutation bias correction, but tree capacity remains
   `PARTIAL`.
3. The time result is a construction check: the simulator duplicates the
   spatial node across stages and appends the true stage coordinate. It shows
   that explicit time preserves that known order, not that temporal stages
   were discovered from text.
4. The near-miss removes the common-ball claim only at the registered
   radius. It supports logical independence between that criterion and
   cue-routing, not the universal statement that every form of intersection
   is unnecessary.
5. The internal hash seal is strong provenance but is not an externally
   immutable preregistration.
