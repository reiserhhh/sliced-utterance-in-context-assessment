# Independent Audit: V8 C2 Topology Margin V1.1

Decision: `PASS_WITH_STRICT_CLAIM_BOUNDARY`

The independent theory and mathematics audit found no confirmation-threshold
reuse, empty-denominator invariance gate, or absolute author-AUC topology gate
that would require stopping V1.1. V1 remains a stopped predecessor; V1.1 is a
new-seed corrected procedure rather than a relabeling of the failed result.

## Evidence accepted

- N=320 clear-world accuracy: mixture 0.970, ring 0.910, curve 0.894.
- N=640 clear-world accuracy: mixture 1.000, ring 0.988, curve 0.926.
- Registered ambiguous primary worlds: refusal 1.000.
- Primary decisive-wrong count: zero; one-sided 95% upper bound 0.00597.
- H1 ring/curve open-set gaps: 0.128, 0.146, 0.201, and 0.234.
- Conditional affine/permutation invariance: 0.974 and 0.982, with baseline
  eligibility 0.924 and 0.969.
- Calibration, primary confirmation, and invariance seed blocks are disjoint.

## Required qualifications

1. The claim is restricted to N at least 320, noise ratio 0.10, gap ratio 4,
   and the registered planted families.
2. Noise ratio 0.25 caused complete refusal, not successful topology recovery.
3. Pearls-on-string recovery at bridge mass 0.10 and 0.20 was only 0.313 and
   0.487. Bridge topology is not validated.
4. Gap 6 accuracy was 0.880, so performance is not monotone in separation.
5. The N=640 curve point estimate passes 0.90, but its Wilson 95% lower bound
   is approximately 0.900 and does not prove population accuracy above 0.90.
6. Affine invariance means invariance after discovery-only whitening, not
   invariance of uncorrected coordinates.
7. Persistent lifetimes are measured on ordinal distance, not the original
   Euclidean scale.
8. Sensitivity scans are diagnostics rather than independent replication.
9. Absolute author AUC is no longer required, but an AUC-gap check remains in
   the discovery-confirmation distribution-matching refusal gate.
10. `minimum_author_matching_auc` is an unused threshold artifact and should
    not be interpreted as a hidden classifier condition.

## Approved claim

> In registered stable C2 response-operator simulations, after discovery-only
> representation fitting and empirical distance-rank normalization, the V1.1
> sample-size-specific open-set rule distinguished separated K-mixtures,
> continuous rings, and continuous curves at N=320 and N=640 under low noise
> and the registered topology margin, while conservatively refusing registered
> null, overlapping, and unstable worlds. This establishes technical topology
> identifiability under those planted conditions only. It does not establish
> real-text topology, personality types, psychological constructs, emotion,
> diagnosis, clinical validity, or pearls-on-string identifiability.
